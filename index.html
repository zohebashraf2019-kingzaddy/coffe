Where espresso meets the spirits shelf

Karajillo & Co. was built around one drink: the Carajillo, espresso and Licor 43 over ice. From there the menu runs both ways, into piloncillo and dulce de leche lattes in the morning and into masa, jamaica and coconut cocktails at night.

The room does the same. Sage cabinets and a checkerboard floor for your morning laptop session, a gold-lit wall of tequila, mezcal and whiskey for when the sun goes down over the river.

From the team behind Toro Bravo, La Condesa and Mexcal.
